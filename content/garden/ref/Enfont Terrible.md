**Enfont Terrible** is an international, ~~award-winning~~ weird type foundry based in Internet and founded by [Javier Arce](https://javier.computer/) just five minutes ago.

We ~~create, publish, and distribute~~ deform, break, and destroy high-quality retail and custom typefaces for ~~analog and digital media~~ fun.

How does it work?

Just drag and drop a font file and move the sliders until you are happy with the result, then click **Create font** and a new font will be generated for you!

**Hint**: Change the preview text by double clicking it and writing a new word.

Licenses

Before using our terrible, terrible foundry, read the license file of the font you intend to use and make sure it allows you this kind of modification.

Example fonts used:

- [IBM Plex Mono](https://github.com/IBM/plex/blob/master/LICENSE.txt)
- [EB Garamond](https://scripts.sil.org/cms/scripts/page.php?site_id=nrsi&id=OFL_web)
- [Roboto Regular](https://scripts.sil.org/cms/scripts/page.php?site_id=nrsi&id=OFL_web)
