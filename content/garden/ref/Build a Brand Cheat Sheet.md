I f you’ve read my crash course brand, on you know what brand is, how it shapes the world, and how to build one for yourself.

**To help you in your brand design journey, here’s a handy list of top resources and tools for each of the** [**deliverables needed to build a stellar brand identity**](https://medium.com/@elanagurney/how-to-build-a-stellar-brand-in-8-steps-97498859da59)**.** May they guide you step by step to success!

## Brand Landscape

### Business Landscape

**Competitive analysis:** curate and analyze competitors’ brand assets and strategies, noting key differentiators  
• [Dig into this comprehensive guide to competitive brand audits](http://www.ignytebrands.com/how-to-do-a-knockout-competitive-brand-audit/)

**Objectives:** clarify the organization’s short- and long-term actionable business goals  
• [Read this overview of the types of objectives to capture](https://www.inc.com/guides/2010/06/setting-business-goals.html)

**Offerings:** clearly define the organization’s products and services  
• [Check out this MIT research on defining business offerings](https://sloanreview.mit.edu/article/designing-the-right-product-offerings/)

**Positioning/value propositions:** outline the benefits the organization delivers, and how this value sets it apart from competitors  
• [Learn how to create value propositions, with examples](https://cxl.com/blog/value-proposition-examples-how-to-create/)

### Messaging Landscape

**One-liner:** create a single sentence that describes the people and problem the organization serves, and its unique solution  
• [Apply these helpful writing suggestions](https://medium.com/@earlydays_io/oneliner-how-to-describe-your-offering-in-one-sentence-dc831a905678)

**Tagline:** select a few words to communicate the brand’s offering and value  
• [Tagline writing tips from 10 entrepreneurs](https://www.inc.com/young-entrepreneur-council/10-tips-for-a-remarkable-tagline.html)

**Boilerplate:** develop a brief, standardized description of what the organization does, including metrics and positioning  
• [Here’s a good overview](https://www.entrepreneur.com/article/229923)

### Audience Landscape

**Audience personas:** build profiles of individuals that represent the behaviors, character, and preferences of a key segment of your audience  
• [Here’s a great 101 on personas](https://www.smashingmagazine.com/2014/08/a-closer-look-at-personas-part-1/), and [tips to uncover customers’ values](https://hbr.org/1998/11/business-marketing-understand-what-customers-value)

**Organizational culture audit:** capture a sense of the team’s values, aspirations, and existing relationship with the brand  
• [Try one of these design research tools](http://designresearchmethods.com/tools/)

## Brand Compass

**Mission statement:** define the core mandate of the organization — what it does, who it serves, and the value it brings to the table  
• [A quick video on writing a mission statement that doesn’t suck](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LJhG3HZ7b4o)

**Vision statement:** clarify where the organization is going and the change it seeks to create in the next 3–5 years  
• [Check out this guide](https://www.projectmanager.com/blog/guide-writing-perfect-vision-statement-examples), or [watch this TED talk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GCgK5M3hY8Y)

**Purpose statement:** answer the fundamental question — why does this organization exist? — and articulate the shared meaning that makes this work worthwhile  
• [How to, and why this differs from your mission/vision](https://hbr.org/2014/09/your-companys-purpose-is-not-its-vision-mission-or-values)

**Values:** name the organization’s core beliefs and the behaviors that will manifest its purpose  
• [Try out these exercises](https://business.tutsplus.com/tutorials/how-to-define-your-core-brand-values-and-why-you-should--cms-26301)

## Visual/Verbal Identity

**Brand Personality**: define the unique character traits of your brand persona to build consistency  
• [Some inspiration from top brands + exercises](https://www.ebaqdesign.com/blog/brand-personality)

**Voice and tone guidelines:** select key words to describe the way your brand communicates, and consider how that changes in context  
• [Peruse voice and tone guidelines from top companies](https://voiceandtoneguides.webflow.io/)

**Editorial style guidelines:** define rules for writing in your brand voice, including grammar, formatting, and a glossary of key terms  
• [Try this useful overview to get started](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/open-ended-questions/)

**Typography:** limit yourself to two typefaces, and try pulling from the same font family  
• [Learn about picking and pairing from typography queen Jessica Hicshe](http://jessicahische.is/talkingtype)

**Colors:** use them with intention and restraint, and consider visual weight and hierarchy  
• [Check out top brand palettes](https://brandcolors.net/), and [this helpful case study](https://medium.com/@pbasu/how-to-choose-a-brand-color-palette-that-fits-your-brand-story-to-a-t-603532af36c2)

**Logo:** create something that looks great across different sizes, materials and contexts  
• [Here’s a detailed guide to designing a great one](https://99designs.com/blog/logo-branding/how-to-design-logo/)

**Creative assets (photography, illustration, iconography etc.):** create a consistent style that is unique but easily scalable  
• [Find some good tips in this article](https://www.eyeem.com/blog/5-tips-to-develop-your-brands-photography-style-guide)

**Multimedia (data visualization, video, sound etc.):** build off your other styles to determine standards you can stick with  
• [See some great data style examples](https://medium.com/nightingale/style-guidelines-92ebe166addc)
