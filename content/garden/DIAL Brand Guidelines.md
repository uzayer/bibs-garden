## Color System (WCAG-checked)
| Role                        | Hex      | On white      | On black     | Use as text?                                                    |
| --------------------------- | -------- | ------------- | ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Primary — Navy              | #062540  | 15.6:1 (AAA)  | —            | Yes, anywhere                                                   |
| Secondary — Blue            | # 144B80 | 8.95:1 (AAA)  | —            | Yes, anywhere                                                   |
| Accent — Orange (bright)    | # F78F28 | 2.36:1 (fail) | 8.91:1 (AAA) | No — graphics/large fills/icons only, never as text on light bg |
| Accent — Orange (deep, new) | # B85E1E | 4.51:1 (AA)   | 4.66:1 (AA)  | Yes, when orange needs to be text or a small UI element         |

Notes:
- Orange on Navy = 6.62:1 (passes AA, not AAA) — safe combo for orange text/labels on dark backgrounds.
- Orange on Blue = 3.80:1 — large text/UI only (≥3:1), not body text.
- Bright orange (#F78F28) is decorative-only under WCAG — reserve for logo, illustrations, large color blocks. Use deep orange (#B85E1E) anywhere orange needs to carry text or pass as a functional UI color.
