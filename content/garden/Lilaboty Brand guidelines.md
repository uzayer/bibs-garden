## Tier 1 — Close open decisions (no new work, just commit)

These are already answered across the files. The task is committing each answer so it stops being an open question.

**1. Fill in the Messaging Landscape section**
Copy from [Lilaboty Generated Ideas](../../Lilaboty%20Generated%20Ideas.md) into the blank fields above:
- Tagline: *"Made with care. Made for you."*
- One-liner: *"Lilaboty is a handcrafted lifestyle brand offering custom traditional Bangladeshi fashion and home decor — made with care, delivered fast, and built with a conscience."*
- Boilerplate: the full paragraph in Generated Ideas → use in Facebook About, Instagram bio, and any event materials

**2. Close the Facebook structure decision**
Decision: one page, split by albums. Albums to create: Sarees, Embroidery Dresses, Crochet, Candles, Trays & Home Decor. Pin the best-performing category album at the top. Add one pinned post explaining how to order (screenshot → DM → confirm → done).

**3. Close the packaging decision**
Start with: kraft paper + logo stamp or sticker. Order 500 rectangular logo stickers from a local Dhaka printer. Use fabric offcuts as wrapping — already on hand, zero cost, on-brand. For candles: design one rectangular wrap-around label in Canva with the candle's emotional name (e.g. *Peace Wave*), the soy wax note, and the logo. Print on kraft sticker paper.

---

## Tier 2 — Nail the visual foundation

Nothing — no templates, no content, no event materials — can be built consistently until palette and typefaces are decided.

**4. Commit to 5 hex codes**
- Primary: `#C05C3A` (burnt terracotta)
- Background: `#F5EDE0` (warm cream)
- Accent: `#D4A020` (mustard gold)
- Nature accent: `#6B7A58` (muted olive)
- Text: `#3C3028` (warm charcoal)

Adjust shades to taste, but pick five and only those. Seasonal extensions (jewel tones for Eid, lighter sand for summer) come after the base palette is locked.

**5. Commit to 2 typefaces**
- Display/headings: Cormorant Garamond (free on Google Fonts)
- Body/captions: DM Sans (free on Google Fonts, clean on mobile)

Both available in Canva. Set them as brand fonts in the Canva workspace so the decision never has to be made again.

---

## Tier 3 — Build the minimum content system

The smallest repeatable system Ammu can sustain alone. Not the full calendar — the floor.

**6. Build 3 Canva templates**
- Clothing product post (portrait, 4:5)
- Home decor/candle post (square, 1:1)
- Story template (9:16) for product reveals or BTS

Each uses the committed palette and typefaces. Ammu swaps the photo and caption only. One afternoon to build all three.

**7. Define the weekly minimum**
One post + one story. That is the floor. The full 4-post weekly calendar (from Generated Ideas) is the ceiling for a good week. The minimum ensures consistency even on low-capacity weeks.

**8. Create the BTS (behind-the-scenes) format**
A recurring format, not freestyle: phone propped up, Ammu's hands working (stitching, pouring wax, block printing), 15–30 seconds, no talking required, ambient or trending audio. Record 5–10 clips in one session and hold them as a buffer. This fills the feed during slow weeks and is the proof of craft that builds brand trust.

---

## Tier 4 — Event design (when a stall is confirmed)

Do not build this until a specific event is booked. When one is, produce in this order:
1. Banner (Canva, printed locally)
2. Branded paper bag (small run at a local press)
3. Price and product labels consistent with the packaging system

---

## What to defer

Pinterest, paid ads, influencer outreach, logo lockup variants, and the full seasonal palette system are all valid long-term moves — after Tiers 1–3 are done. The brand currently has more strategy than execution. Close the open decisions before making new ones.

**The single most unblocking action:** paste the tagline and messaging into the Messaging Landscape section above. Five minutes. Closes the biggest open loop in the project.
